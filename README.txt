BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Confirm that “Astro Attorneys” is the final brand name. “Attorneys” commonly means legal professionals and may confuse visitors.
2. Confirm the business email and WhatsApp number shown on the page are the official ones.
3. Set the final public URL in index.html and add absolute og:image / og:url tags so link previews show the og-image.jpg picture.
4. Confirm the services Abirami actually offers; remove any that do not apply.
5. Confirm languages, consultation modes, duration, fees, working hours and cancellation/refund policy.
6. Add a privacy policy explaining how birth details and enquiries are collected, used, stored and deleted. Do not request sensitive birth details through an unsecured public form.
7. Add only genuine client testimonials with the clients’ permission.
8. Confirm that “25 years of experience” is the preferred public wording and keep evidence for any credentials or qualifications you choose to advertise.

HOW TO PREVIEW:
Open index.html in Chrome. For a public launch, upload the complete folder to a static host such as Netlify or Vercel, or connect it to a domain.

This is a front-end starter, not a production booking/payment system. It does not store enquiries or process payments.
