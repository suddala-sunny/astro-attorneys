Jyotish Vidya

Bharatiya Jyotisha · Heritage

An ancient Indian science of time and the sky.

Jyotisha, “the science of light”, is one of the six Vedangas, the traditional limbs of the Vedas. For centuries scholars recorded their calculations in Sanskrit and Tamil on palm leaf and paper, and temples across South India honour the nine Grahas. These illustrations celebrate that living heritage.

Reading a chart

The South Indian Rasi chart

Tamil Jothidam commonly uses a square chart in which the twelve Rashis keep fixed places: Meena sits at top left and the signs run clockwise. The planets are then written into the sign they occupy at the moment of birth.
